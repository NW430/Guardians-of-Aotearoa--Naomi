Protecting Our Whenua

Aotearoa New Zealand is home to unique forests, rivers, oceans, plants and animals. Kaitiakitanga encourages us to care for these taonga and protect them for future generations.

Learn More

Why Does It Matter?

Our Forests

Native forests provide homes for many species and help protect our soil and waterways.

Our Wildlife

Aotearoa has many unique native species that need protection from introduced predators and habitat loss.

Our Water

Rivers, lakes and oceans are important ecosystems that need to be protected from pollution and other threats.

Be a Kaitiaki

Everyone can make a difference. Small actions such as reducing waste, protecting native plants and keeping waterways clean can help protect Aotearoa.
